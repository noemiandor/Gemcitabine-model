# Figure Legends

## Figure 7. Tumor growth and growth inhibition, transcriptional states, and endpoint copy-number architecture in gemcitabine-treated SUM-159 xenografts

**(A)** Change in tumor volume (mm³) from the first treatment day (Day 0) for 16 tumors initiated with near-diploid (2N) or near-tetraploid (4N) SUM-159 cells. Each injected-origin group comprised four vehicle-treated tumors and two tumors at each gemcitabine dose (30 and 120 mg/kg). Thin lines represent individual mice; gray, orange, and green denote vehicle, 30 mg/kg, and 120 mg/kg, respectively. Dashed black lines are the mean trajectories of the vehicle-treated mice matched by injected origin, and the vertical red line marks Day 17. Open circles mark individual-mouse Day-17 values; red diamonds mark the corresponding matched-vehicle means. For each treated mouse, Day-17 tumor growth inhibition (TGI) was calculated as $100\times[1-\Delta V_{\mathrm{treated}}/\overline{\Delta V}_{\mathrm{matched\ vehicle}}]$, where $\Delta V$ is the change in volume from Day 0 to Day 17.

**(B)** Day-17 TGI for the eight treated tumors, grouped by injected ploidy origin ($n=4$ tumors per group). Blue and pink boxes denote 2N- and 4N-origin tumors, respectively; orange circles and green triangles denote 30 and 120 mg/kg, respectively. Boxes show the median and interquartile range; whiskers extend to the most extreme observation within 1.5 times the interquartile range, and all independent tumors are plotted. The dose-adjusted 4N-minus-2N difference was $-39.5$ percentage points (exact permutation test stratified by dose, $P=0.0556$).

**(C)** Joint uniform manifold approximation and projection (UMAP) of 35,513 cells—9,832 final quality-controlled (QC) tumor cells from 16 tumors and 25,681 cells from two cultured SUM-159 reference preparations—colored by the nine final curated transcriptional clusters. Color-matched cluster-number labels mark cluster locations and provide the cluster-color key.

**(D)** The same UMAP colored by injected origin for tumor cells and preparation ploidy for cultured-reference cells, with blue indicating 2N and vermilion indicating 4N, as shown in the in-panel key.

**(E)** The same UMAP colored by source context, with blue indicating xenograft tumor cells and yellow indicating cultured reference cells, as shown in the in-panel key.

**(F)** Mean within-sample cluster proportions for the 16 tumors and two cultured reference samples. Proportions were calculated separately within each biological sample and then averaged with equal sample weight within each context, irrespective of the number of sequenced cells; blue and yellow bars denote tumors and cultured references, respectively. An asterisk above a cluster marks positive enrichment of the context represented by the taller bar ($*$, panel-wide Benjamini–Hochberg $q\leq0.05$). Enrichment was assessed by an exact one-sided context-label permutation of sample-level proportions within 2N/4N strata, with Benjamini–Hochberg correction across all context-by-cluster contrasts; asterisks do not mark depletion.

**(G)** Cluster-versus-rest Hallmark gene-set enrichment analysis for the nine-cluster landscape. The 20 gene sets with the largest maximum absolute normalized enrichment score (NES) across clusters are shown; rows and columns are hierarchically clustered independently. Red and blue indicate positive and negative NES, respectively. Values displayed as white zero tiles are placeholders for cluster–pathway combinations lacking a finite NES; they do not denote nonsignificance, and this heatmap does not encode false-discovery rate.

**(H)** Mouse-balanced empirical cumulative distribution functions (ECDFs) of cell-cycle-associated pseudotime for 2,881 tumor cells in clusters 4c, 6, and 10 across the 16 tumors (1,328 vehicle-treated and 1,553 gemcitabine-treated cells, with the two gemcitabine doses pooled). An ECDF was calculated separately for each mouse and then averaged across mice so that each mouse contributed equally. Gray and blue curves denote vehicle and gemcitabine treatment, respectively; solid curves show the combined and 2N-origin comparisons, and dashed curves show the 4N-origin comparison. The combined comparison included eight mice per group (root-mean-square error [RMSE] $=0.0378$; exact treatment-label permutation of the mouse-level ECDFs, $P=0.00513$); the 4N- and 2N-origin comparisons each included four mice per group (RMSE $=0.0317$, $P=0.143$, and RMSE $=0.0456$, $P=0.0571$, respectively).

**(I)** Model-estimated leading-edge gene-expression profiles across cell-cycle-associated pseudotime for the same 2,881-cell subset. Mouse-aware pseudobulk models included dose and injected origin; 14 mice had analyzable bins within the marked 0.296–0.486 interval. This interval was taken directly from the maximal connected positive density-contrast region with nominal two-sided pointwise permutation support in Supplementary Fig. 4I; dashed lines mark its exact boundaries. Expression within it was contrasted with that in the two equally wide adjacent intervals, [0.106,0.296) and (0.486,0.676]. Within each Hallmark, Reactome, or Gene Ontology Biological Process (GO BP) collection, up to four positively and four negatively enriched pathways were selected only when they passed collection-wide Benjamini–Hochberg $q\leq0.05$; nonsignificant pathways were not substituted when fewer than four passed. For each pathway, the fitted trajectory of each leading-edge gene was standardized across pseudotime before the standardized trajectories were averaged. Red and blue therefore indicate relatively higher and lower mean standardized fitted expression, respectively. Enrichment direction compares the selected interval with its adjacent states and is not a direct treatment effect at fixed pseudotime; pathway inference is conditional on the data-selected interval boundaries.

**(J)** NUMBAT-derived cell-by-chromosome copy-number profiles for the exact 9,832-cell final-QC tumor universe used in the transcriptional analyses (4,880 2N-origin and 4,952 4N-origin cells from all 16 mice; 5,335 treated). Rows are cells, columns are chromosomes 1–22, and each tile is the length-weighted mean segmental copy-number state for that cell and chromosome. Mouse blocks retain a fixed injected-origin, dose, and mouse order; cells are hierarchically clustered separately within each mouse, while chromosomes remain in genomic order. The matrix scale runs from blue for lower states through gold for copy number 3 to red and purple for higher states. From the matrix outward, annotation bars denote injected origin (2N, blue; 4N, vermilion), dose (vehicle, gray; 30 mg/kg, orange; 120 mg/kg, green), and mouse, with mouse colors defined in the accompanying key. The two origin-specific NUMBAT segment schemas are shown together at chromosome resolution but were not locus-aligned across runs.

**(K)** Association across the eight treated tumors between Day-17 TGI and pseudotime-distribution displacement, measured as ECDF RMSE from the equal-mouse vehicle reference matched by injected origin; both variables were centered within dose. Each point is one mouse; text labels identify mice, orange and green indicate 30 and 120 mg/kg, and circles and triangles indicate 2N- and 4N-origin tumors, respectively. The black line is the linear fit and the gray band its 95% confidence interval (Pearson $r=0.740$; exact within-dose permutation of TGI labels, $P=0.0174$).

**(L)** Unadjusted association across the same eight treated tumors between Day-17 TGI and mean endpoint tumor-cell ploidy. NUMBAT-derived estimates were restricted to the 5,335 treated cells within the 9,832-cell final-QC tumor universe before calculating one mean per mouse. Point labels, colors, and shapes are as in K; the black line is the linear fit and the gray band its 95% confidence interval (Pearson $r=-0.698$; exact unrestricted permutation of TGI labels, $P=0.0591$). Because the 2N- and 4N-origin cohorts were processed in separate NUMBAT runs with different segment schemas and calibration, this association is descriptive and does not establish an origin- or dose-independent endpoint-ploidy effect. TGI, tumor growth inhibition; UMAP, uniform manifold approximation and projection; ECDF, empirical cumulative distribution function; NES, normalized enrichment score; GO BP, Gene Ontology Biological Process.
